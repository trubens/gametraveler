# Map surface (src/App.tsx)

Scope: the whole single-page map app: masthead, filter, map, markers, location panel. Visitor mode: Operate (explore and reminisce on a map).

Audience/job: gamers 30–60 browsing where games happen, recognizing favorites, following a game across its locations. Phone and desktop.

Constraints: no cover art or screenshots exist; do not invent any. Norwegian UI copy. Large text and touch targets.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The map is a fold-out supplement from a 1990s games magazine; every panel is a feature spread. Refuses the default floating-search map app with soft cards and blue pins.

OWN-WORLD: Glossy white stock and ink black; tiles printed in one grey ink (desaturated); CMYK process inks: magenta as the single action/route color, cyan, yellow, black for genre tags. Archivo variable: condensed heavy caps headlines, normal-width UI. Hairline-to-heavy ink rules instead of shadows; square corners.

STORY: Visitor sees the whole world dotted in magenta, searches or taps a cluster, reads a spread of games at that place (year first), taps a game to see its route drawn across the map and travels on.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black masthead strip with condensed GAME TRAVELLER, live issue line (count, year span), search inline as the primary action, Filter button with active count. Below: full-bleed grey map with magenta dots and black numbered cluster bullets. Panel docks left on desktop (400px), bottom sheet on mobile.

FORM: Retro games-magazine spread, candidate 4 of 7 grounded list, seed dd9c04a9. Raises: route line burning brightest (from transit diagram); seams as ink rules not shadow (from dark console).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
